Community Rules

Official rules for Orion Drift Pickleball.

  • Only one bounce

    The ball can only hit the ground once. If the ball hits the ground more than once, the point goes to the opposing team.

  • Only the Orion Edge or VRML Paddles

    This is to maintain fairness; "pay to win" paddles (like the Skel.EXE bundle, which has a bigger hitbox) are not allowed. Compromises will be considered in the future.

  • Serve underhand only

    Serves must always be made with an underhand motion. Overhand serves are not allowed.

  • Diagonal serving

    The ball must be served diagonally into the opponent’s service box. A straight-across serve is a fault.

  • Stay out of the kitchen

    You cannot volley (hit the ball before it bounces) while standing inside the “kitchen” area near the net. You must let the ball bounce first if you are in the kitchen.

  • Points are scored on serve

    Only the serving side can score points. If the receiving team wins the rally, they gain the serve instead of the point.

  • Win by 2

    A game is normally played to 11 points, but you must win by at least 2 points (e.g., 11–9, 12–10, etc.).

  • Small ball only

    You may only use the small heartball, and not the big/default one.
